# Ecana Energy fuel migration (February 2026 onward)

Source: the sibling `petrol-station-app` MongoDB. Target: a new CaZone organization with slug `ecana-energy`, one `fuel_station` service, and the three current branches EE1 Jikwoyi, EE3 Guzape, and EE2 New Nyanya. The existing Ecana Family organization is separate.

The owner confirmed a 2026-02-01 UTC cutoff, including all February operating history. The 2026-10-05 read-only dry run found 345 closed shifts (first 2026-02-04), 2,824 sales, 2,935 meter readings, 2,881 tank-stock entries, 202 stock movements, 268 cash deposits, 43 payment records, 40 price changes, 135 attendant assignments, and 312 audit logs. Seven `2000-01-01` placeholder meters are before the cutoff. Source data can still change, so rerun the dry run at cutover.

All 2,824 source sales link to a saved shift and meter. At the 2026-10-05 18:07 UTC dry run, 2,735 would become operational sales. Fifty-five saved sales had recent unapproved meters; another 34 saved sales had invalid or zero financial values or disagreed with their meters. Those 89 meters would remain incomplete, without sales or debt. Another 100 older unmatched meters would also remain incomplete. Eleven recent unapproved unmatched meters would be archived only. The Summary Book and Incomplete Historical Pump Records page show the 189 incomplete rows. A cashier may submit corrected readings and source evidence after shift closure using a one-time code; a manager then checks the historical tank, product, price, and evidence before approving an order and stock movement. The original source document remains archived and both actions are audited. Only eight of the 43 payment records have manager approval **and** a positive received amount. The other 35 are retained in the source archive; age alone does not prove money was collected or create attendant debt. Older pumps without approved collections are marked `unknown` in CaZone.

Historical station IDs `6a306aa5ebcd1418d8ec556f` and `697ef6d85a9ad0e86a84e2fd` are explicitly mapped into current Guzape and New Nyanya respectively, following the owner's choice to merge by matching station name. Three other missing station IDs appear only in audit history, so their original IDs are kept in the archive. Jikwoyi tank `PMS2` and two removed Guzape tanks retain their historical identity as inactive tanks. The dry run confirmed all 2,980 saved shift tank mappings resolve.

The historical data was imported on 2026-10-05 in `--history-only` mode into organization `cmuvkik59000010fyecdhpu8b`. The `fuel_station` service is inactive with `migrationStockPending: true`, and there are no stocktake moves. The latest saved closing dips are not manager approved and differ substantially from MongoDB's mutable `currentStock` fields. ## Cutover sequence

1. Keep every old-app shift closed and freeze new source entries. The 2026-10-05 import found all 345 shifts closed.
2. Stop new entries in the old app and take a manager-signed reading of every active tank. Fill [ecana-cutover-stock.template.json](./ecana-cutover-stock.template.json) with ISO timestamps, approver name, and litres. Keep the signed source document outside Git; pass its path to the importer.
3. Run `node scripts/preflight-ecana-fuel.mjs` and `node scripts/import-ecana-fuel.mjs --cutover-file=PATH` from CaZone. The latter is dry-run unless `--apply` is present. Resolve every blocker and review the source counts against a frozen source snapshot.
4. The additive PostgreSQL migration and history-only import have already been applied. Run the importer again with `--apply --cutover-file=PATH` to post the signed stocktake and activate the petrol service. Stable source-derived IDs and `skipDuplicates` make this resumable.
5. Verify source-to-target counts again, branch prices, per-pump unknown/verified collection status, closing dips, go-live stock per product, and representative historical days before switching daily operation to CaZone.

## Mapping and limits

The importer creates CaZone users (retaining their bcrypt password hashes), branches, fuel products, current and historical tanks, pumps, attendants, shifts, confirmed meter sales, orders, stock moves, approved collections, deposits, receipt deliveries, dips, prices, and audit entries. The live verification confirmed all 12 hash matches, all 11 station-bound user mappings, and zero cross-organization email/username collisions. Source documents from every MongoDB collection are also stored by collection and source ID in `LegacyFuelRecord`, excluding the user password hash from that archive. This keeps fields without direct CaZone equivalents available for reconciliation. On 2026-10-06, the imported owner credentials signed in successfully on `https://cazone-platform.vercel.app` after production deployed commit `6d6a28a`. The historical dashboard, summary book, and subscription page returned 200; the construction-materials page returned 404; and an attempted live shift start returned 409 before any write.

Source `sale` stock movements are archived but are not posted again to CaZone's stock ledger: eligible saved SalesEntry records generate one sale move each. Source payment records that are pending or zero value are archived without treating them as received cash. The 72-hour rule imports 2,847 mature or manager-confirmed tank dips and leaves 34 recent unconfirmed dips in the archive only, as of the stated dry run. An imported dip is a historical measurement, not evidence of manager approval. Historical physical tank capacity absent from current settings is recorded as unknown (`0`) on an inactive historical tank. Late approved sale corrections add an offset at the signed cutover time so verified present-day on-hand stock is preserved.

One source receipt (`6a317b59292e0eb099593054`) has an exact cost of ₦39,102,000, which exceeds CaZone's 32-bit kobo field for `Delivery.totalCost`. Its full source document is archived and its 29,400 litres are included in the stock ledger; no false operational cost was written. The other 180 receipts became operational deliveries.

The small non-fuel MongoDB commerce collections (for example, source `orders`, `transactions`, and `topups`) are preserved in `LegacyFuelRecord`; they do not become new CaZone shop transactions as part of this **fuel** migration. A separate mapping is needed if those workflows must become operational in CaZone.
